Pac-Manhattan

You're the ghost now. Something yellow and hungry is hunting you through the streets of New York.

  1. Move by pointing your cursor where you want to go, or use the arrow keys / WASD. You stay on real New York streets.
  2. Find the place named in your task. It glows gold with a beam of light once it's in view.
  3. Visit ★ landmarks for points, a photo and fun facts, and a hint: how far the target is, and which way.
  4. Don't get chomped. The chomper gets faster with every task you finish.

Up is uptown, right is east. P to pause, M to mute.
