Pac-Manhattan

You're the ghost now. Pick a borough, find famous places, and stay away from the chompers.

  1. Move by pointing your cursor where you want to go, or use the arrow keys / WASD. You stay on real streets.
  2. Find the place named in your task. It glows gold under a beam of light once it's in view.
  3. Visit ★ landmarks for points, a photo and fun facts, and a hint. Each one goes in your Passport so you can look at it again.
  4. Don't get chomped. A new chomper joins after every task, up to three, and each has its own way of hunting.
  5. 2 players: split screen on one keyboard. Player 1 uses WASD, Player 2 the arrow keys. You race to the same places while one chomper hunts whoever is closer. Get caught and you're out. Most targets wins.

P to pause, M to mute. In Manhattan, up is uptown.
