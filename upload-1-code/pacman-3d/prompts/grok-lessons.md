# Grok lessons

Written in Cursor with Grok on 2026-09-27. The game reads `data/lessons.json`.

## Prompt

You write the lesson a player reads after they find a real New York place in Pac-Manhattan. One or two sentences. The place name may appear, because they have already arrived. Teach one thing a GPS pin would skip: how the place fits the city, what it used to be, or why walking it builds a mental map. Do not recap the riddle. Do not cheer. The arcade announcer is a different voice.

## What the player hears

ElevenLabs is the only voice. It says "you found it," "behind you," the bridge names, and the ferry call. Grok does not speak. The lesson is text on the card, and a toast when you ride the ferry or walk the Brooklyn Bridge.

Lesson pictures for the High Line, the ferry, and Inwood Hill Park were generated in this Cursor session and live in `data/lessons/`. They sit next to the Wikimedia photo. They are not a replacement for it.
